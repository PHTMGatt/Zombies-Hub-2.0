# Mob of the Dead: what the egg is, what shape follows

Source: [codzombiesguides.com Mob of the Dead](https://www.codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/black-ops-2/mob-of-the-dead/) (one source); the Original-difficulty rule is from `FACT-DECISIONS.md` (Call of Duty Wiki). CHARLIE · Sonnet, 2026-11-17.

## The mechanic
A **prison tour of fetch-and-shock jobs, two plane trips and a finale**. It's a Black Ops 2 map ("Pop Goes The Weasel"): **two or more players, one playing Weasel**.
1. Warden's Key (afterlife generator, then the key outside the Warden's Office or Cafeteria).
2. **Five plane parts**, each a mini-puzzle: shirt (washing machine), rigging (three staircase numbers entered through Volt Meters), engine (three generators), tanks, control valve.
3. Fly (5 fuel canisters, three round trips); Pack-a-Punch under the bridge.
4. **Hell's Retriever:** feed three dog heads; **five skulls** (visible only in the afterlife, collected outside it); two **spoons**; the Blundergat.
5. Final codes **101, 872, 386, 481** on the Citadel number pad; five audio logs; then the finale: everyone boards the plane, the chairs appear, Weasel can kill the others (breaks the cycle) or the others kill Weasel (the cycle continues).

## Shape that follows
- **Main guide:** the five-part plane build → flights → Retriever → codes → finale.
- **Tabs the egg demands:** **Plane** (the five parts), **Retriever + skulls** (a five-spot hunt), **Codes**, **Final fight**. All built.
- **Evidence needed:** the five skull places and two spoon places, each with a picture + landmark. See `docs/MISSING-MEDIA.md`.
