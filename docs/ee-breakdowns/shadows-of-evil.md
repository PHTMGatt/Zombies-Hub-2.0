# Shadows of Evil: what the egg is, what shape follows

Source: [codzombiesguides.com Shadows of Evil](https://www.codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/black-ops-3/shadows-of-evil/) (one source). Bryen: "middle ground of everything; many run styles. Later." CHARLIE · Sonnet, 2026-11-17.

## The mechanic
**Four players, four districts, four characters**, running largely in parallel:
1. Summoning Key from the box on the truck in Spawn (beast-mode melee).
2. Steps 1–3 in any order: four **rituals** (Spawn: Lawyer's Pen; Canals: Detective's Badge; Footlight: Wig; Waterfront: Champion's Belt), one at a time, each yielding a **Gateworm**; the **second and fourth completed ritual** each spawn a Margwa. Four Gateworms on four pedestals → final ritual → Shadowman and Pack-a-Punch.
3. **Symbols:** train rides show two each; three red symbols out of nine (**random per game**); shock the three on the underground wall → Apothicon Eggs and Swords.
4. **Eggs:** each player fills an egg on a Cthulhu statue (10 zombies; four statues hidden in boxes broken in beast mode) → **Arch-Ovum** from their own district → four red circles → up to four Margwas; one ritual per round.
5. Capture the Flag (Nero's book, purple electricity spots, one flag per round).
6. Shadowman: Keepers' Reborn Swords drop his shield, capture with the Key. Final: endless purple-headed Margwas, cleanse the pink aura with a white wisp; three players shock train-station boxes while the fourth shocks Keepers.

## Shape that follows
- **Main guide:** the sequence, but the district work is **per player and parallel**, so group by character/district rather than one long list. This is the map where "which character am I" changes the page.
- **Tabs the egg demands:** only Step details exists today. The egg suggests **Rituals** (four districts: item + where), **Symbols** (nine-symbol key + train rides) and **Final fight** (four player roles): small fixed tables, little hunt media.
- Open facts (FACT-DECISIONS): ritual length; the source ties Margwas to the 2nd and 4th ritual, our step names the Footlight (4th).
