# Gorod Krovi: what the egg is, what shape follows

Source: [codzombiesguides.com Gorod Krovi](https://www.codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/black-ops-3/gorod-krovi/) (one source) plus Bryen's brief. Method: the egg shapes the guide. Structure only, no design. CHARLIE · Sonnet, 2026-11-17.

## The mechanic
A **long chain of unlike jobs**, one hard puzzle, and a **location hunt** (six trophies) in the middle:
1. Power the Hatchery generator (Valkyrie Drone). The source gives a ~5-minute countdown; our pages say "about 5 minutes".
2. Dragon Network Controller: three code cylinders (Blue, Yellow, Red order), each triggering a Groph Pod defence of ~15 zombies.
3. **Valve puzzle:** six valves. The green-light valve and the whistling (pink cylinder) valve are **random each game**; set the other valves, return the cylinder to S.O.P.H.I.A., enter **KRONOS** on the letter wheel.
4. Dragon Egg → Gauntlet of Siegfried (napalm, penetration, melee kills, incubate in the Hatchery); Dragon Strike from a four-wave lockdown.
5. **Six trophies (hunt)**, each with its own trigger, then six challenges at Dragon Command (Information Download always last). The **Bomb Challenge** means memorising the flashed area order and defusing: ~60 s co-op, ~3 min solo.
6. Power core → launch the Welp → boss: Dragon (weak point, four hits), then the Nikolai mech (four weak points, then the centre).
Works at any player count.

## Bryen's brief vs the source
"8 Samantha steps, then the bomb step": the source calls her S.O.P.H.I.A. and puts the Bomb Challenge **inside the six challenges**, not as a separate second part. His "wrong switch because a location was given by name" lesson is the valves and the bomb step: those need a picture + landmark, never a name.
