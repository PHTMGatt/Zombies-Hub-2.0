# Zetsubou No Shima: what the egg is, what shape follows

Source: [codzombiesguides.com Zetsubou No Shima](https://www.codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/black-ops-3/zetsubou-no-shima/) (one source). Bryen's brief: "like Revelations, a parts hunt all over the map". CHARLIE · Sonnet, 2026-11-17.

## The mechanic
A **parts-and-tools hunt**, then a **three-cog** build, then a boss:
1. Reveal the elevator machinery: the Skull of Nan Sapwe (four skulls cleansed at the Trials altar; every player finishes their own Trials of the Ancients; Keepers take the skull) mesmerises two walls.
2. **Three cogs:** Cog 1 (Anywhere But Here is mandatory, cog above Lab A); Cog 2 (seed, Blue Water, KT-4 or Masamune shot each round for three rounds → flak shell → Flak 88 at the cargo plane, which passes about every two minutes); Cog 3 (electrified Zombie Shield; every player must have done their Trials).
3. **Four tools, each a three-part hunt:** KT-4 (Yellow Vial, Spider Venom, Plant), Masamune (Liquid Divinium Vial, giant-spider fang, Plant + Rainbow Water for three rounds), Zombie Shield (Handles, Frame, Face Screen), Gas Mask (Strap, Cover, Breathing Tube).
4. Boss: elevator below the Bunker; the **Masamune player** shoots time-gated weak points on the captive Giant Thrasher; survive the waves.
Any player count; the Trials part is per player.

## Shape that follows
- **Main guide:** the four phases (reveal → cogs → elevator → boss).
- **Tabs the egg demands:** Reveal Blueprint, **Cogs 1/2/3** (one page each: each is its own puzzle), **Buildables** (the hunt: 12 parts, each needs a landmark + picture), Elevator Battle, Prerequisites (Trials; Masamune/KT-4 needed). All built.
- **Evidence needed:** the twelve part places plus Seed Planter, Blue/Rainbow Water, Spider cage, Lab B cage. See `docs/MISSING-MEDIA.md`.
- Keep out until sourced: "20 Keepers solo" (see `FACT-DECISIONS.md`).
