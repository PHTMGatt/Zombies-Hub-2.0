# Accessibility audit, 2026-11-16 (findings only)

Scope: hub pages and all seven map guides, every internal sub-page linked from each map (88 route × viewport runs at 1440×900 and 390×844). Tools: axe-core 4.10.2 (WCAG 2 A/AA, 2.1 A/AA, best practice) plus a keyboard Tab walk (28 stops each on home, Origins, Revelations, Gorod Krovi). Run against the dev server on the working tree at `8321bd3` plus others' uncommitted edits. Nothing was changed. Owners: ALPHA for markup/CSS, CHARLIE for scripts.

## Passed

- **No axe errors** for colour contrast, form labels, link or button names, image alt text, duplicate ids, lang, document title or ARIA validity on any of the 88 runs.
- **Focus visible**: every one of the 112 focus stops had a visible outline or ring.
- **Tab order** follows reading order on home, Origins, Revelations and Gorod: skip link, brand, nav, page nav, steps in order, then the completion buttons. Revelations steps 01–12 are in numeric order.
- **Skip link** ("Skip to guide") is the first stop on every page I walked.

## Not covered

- **Contrast over images and gradients**: axe can't judge text on art. A pixel-sample pass would be needed, mostly Revelations and Gorod headers.
- **Real screen reader** (NVDA/VoiceOver) behaviour, 200% zoom reflow, forced colours, reduced motion.
- Tab order was walked on four pages only; the other three maps and Side EEs were axe-only.
- The lightbox, tab panels and Valve Solver controls were not driven by keyboard.
