## Navigation (every map)

- **Edge fade on swipe strips**: the fade shows only on the side that has more items past it, and nothing fades when the strip fits. Revelations tab row, Origins staff chips and Speed Run rail, Mob and Gorod nav [6f74614].
- **Visible keyboard focus**: Gorod nav focus is a 2 px outline (it looked the same as the active tab), Valve Solver select ring and `/maps` search ring are stronger [f5acd40]. Tab through them with the keyboard.

## Revelations

- **Step picker**: on a narrow desktop window (641–850 px, mouse) the 12-step strip now wraps into a grid so every step is reachable. Touch keeps the swipe strip [0744735]. Resize a desktop window to ~800 px.
- **Landscape phone**: title and intro share one line, the step strip sits 42 px higher, step chips are wider so no title is cut [0744735]. Check at 844×390.
- **Hunt pages**: labels bigger (kickers 10.6 → 11.8 px, video label 9.9 → 11.5 px, option meta 11.5 → 12.5 px) [d52f713].
- **Step buttons** are 44 px tall (were 43) [7687d13].
- **Original split guide layout** is back: left step rail on desktop, step strip on phone, one focused step at a time, current references kept [446ee1c, merged from PR #52].
- **Blocked YouTube** (ad blocker, school/work network): after 10 s a "Player unavailable" note shows and the YouTube link stays [80671c7].
- **Deep links**: opening a step by `#link` no longer yanks you back after you've scrolled or tapped [4c7d30a].
